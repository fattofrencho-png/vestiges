# Vestiges — Codex flavour text (Batch 5 draft, for approval)

Two lines per entry: line 1 at Discovered (Bestiary: 10 kills or first sighting for peaceful mobs), line 2 at Mastered (Bestiary: 250 kills).
Rules: one sentence per line, 20 words max · a curious traveller's notebook (quotes villagers and faiths, never knows the full truth) · a mix of tips (true in game), villager sayings, faith views and lore hints (about 1 entry in 5) · light humour, no modern words · shaped blocks (stairs, slabs, walls…) and colour variants share their base entry · generated places get a line built from their name and type.
Note: lines for 26.3's newest content (sulfur caves, cinnabar, poplar, parched, camel husk, nautilus, sulfur cube…) stay vague on mechanics until checked in game during the Codex milestone.
