# Mob origins

The RPG explanation of the vanilla mobs, so nothing feels out of place in the world. Discussed mob by mob with the author; only approved cards are saved here. The existing Codex lines in `codex-flavour.md` stay as they are.

## The three levels

1. **Fluff:** what the people of our fantasy world think the mob is. The folk view.
2. **Flavour:** a hint about the real origin.
3. **Lore:** the reality of the mob's existence. Shown in the Codex only after the 24 dragons are killed. The Kesmar (the author's "Makers") may be named here.

Not every mob gets every level. Minor animals may get only level 1, or nothing. Texts are short and poetic: the player never reads the truth plainly.

## Cards

### Zombie
- **Where / when:** spawns in the dark, at night, in caves and dungeon spawners; joins raids and village sieges; burns in sunlight unless it wears a helmet; turns villagers into zombie villagers.
- **Current Codex:** line 1 "Slow, stubborn, always hungry. Burns at dawn unless it finds a hat." · line 2 "The Promised call them 'the uncounted'."
- **L1 Fluff:** "Nobody mourns a zombie. The old ones say it earned its walk."
- **L2 Flavour:** "Of all things, it fears the sun, as a thief fears being seen."
- **L3 Lore:** "Each one broke a rule the Kesmar wrote into the world. They do not hunger for flesh. They hunger for the mercy they were denied."
- **Ties:** cursed by the Kesmar for a Sin committed in life, wrong for the world, probably alchemy (never said outright). The sun is how the Kesmar watch. Same curse as Zombie Villager (more recent, soul not gone), Husk and Drowned (shaped by where the Sin was done).
- **Status:** approved 2026-10-30

### Zombie Villager
- **Where / when:** born when zombies kill villagers (Normal and Hard); also found in some structures; can be cured with Weakness and a golden apple, then trades much better.
- **Current Codex:** line 1 "Cure it with a splash of Weakness and a golden apple." · line 2 "Cured, it remembers you, and trusts you completely." (Golden Apple line 2: "Half of a zombie villager's cure.")
- **L1 Fluff:** "A zombie villager still knows your face. That is what makes it so sad to meet."
- **L2 Flavour:** "It groans in the old voice. Part of someone is still in there, listening."
- **L3 Lore:** "Its wrong was done only yesterday, so the Kesmar's grip is still loose. Weakness and gold loosen it more, and the soul finds the way home."
- **Ties:** same curse as Zombie, but the Sin is recent, so part of the soul is left and the cure works.
- **Status:** approved 2026-10-30

## Group decisions (author's answers in the review stream, 2026-10-30)

- **Mounts:** beasts share the curse of the rider they would not leave (zombie horse, zombie nautilus, camel husk).
- **Drowned:** both. The Tidefolk guarded, and some sinned by touching what they guarded.
- **Allay:** stay vague for now. No search for Sift mobs (author, 2026-10-30).
- **Skeletons:** the oldest Sins, with the flesh gone; the variants follow where the Sin was done. The arrows never run out because the Kesmar's debt is never paid.
- **Brewers:** their alchemy (Nitox) was the Sin of the swamp. It touches Bogged and Witch. Witches are the Brewers' last heirs.
- **Creeper:** an alchemist's failed work that learned to want. Cats can see the Kesmar's tools, so creepers and phantoms fear them.
- **Spider:** old wildlife, corrupted and then abandoned. Not cursed, not the Deepdelvers' silk farms (author settled on "abandoned", 2026-10-30).
- **Slime:** a leftover of the world's edits (Dhars).
- **Enderman:** the Taken (Dreen). **Phantom:** the night teaching the Sleepless to sleep (Ilvig). **Ender Dragon:** Fluff and Flavour only; the dragon chapters and the Vestige carry its truth.
- **Illagers:** pillager, vindicator and evoker share one origin, the remnants of the Hollow Kings' people (Hergun). **Vex:** the unfinished dead, called back while the evoker holds the spell.
- **Nether:** piglins and hoglins are natives of a place made later. Ghasts are the sparks of the fire the Kilnfolk saw (Belaz). The Happy Ghast is a hint of the Sift. Crossing the edge means the law that reads the dead reads zoglins and zombified piglins too.
- **Old guardians:** the Warden is the Deep Ones' guard (Selince). The Creaking moves unseen like the Kesmar's edits. The Sniffer carries seeds of earlier cycles, hidden against the purge. The Iron Golem was built by the Last Guard's heirs (Tenrys). The Copper Golem needs a freedom hint, because Dilecta's factory runs on enslaved ones.
- **People:** Villager Lore stays hidden (it is the spine). The Wandering Trader is, in the author's words, "a traveller from around the world, with his own knowledge". Not from the Sift, not a Vitera courier.
- **Wildlife:** the Cat sees the watchers. The Cow keeps its Brewers' sickness hint in the Codex as it is now.
