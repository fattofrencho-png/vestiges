# Vestiges — shrine and dungeon fragments (Batch 5 draft, for approval)

Rules: 2–4 sentences, 60 words max · a real voice (diary, prayer, letter, carving) · the truth is never said outright · each hides one detail linking to a dragon chapter · lore names keep the anagram rule · no modern words.

## Ruined shrines (40)

### Primacy (10)
1. **Flame prayer:** "Flame, eat what is rotten and spare what is sound. Burn our pride before it grows. We will rebuild from your ash, as our fathers did, and theirs before them."
2. **Tide basin:** "When the water rises, do not climb. The Tide takes only the proud." (carved on the basin)
3. **Stone prayer:** "Stone remembers what we forget. Lay your hand on the shrine and ask it what stood here before the village. It will not answer. It never answers. But it is warm."
4. **Gale prayer:** "Gale, carry our names above the clouds, so that whoever listens up there knows we kept the old ways."
5. **On the Turning:** "Elder Maud taught that the Turning comes when the four fall out of balance. I asked her who keeps the scales. She sent me to sweep the shrine for a week."
6. **Keeper of Stone:** "The miners of Thornner say the deep rock rings like a bell before a Turning. My uncle heard it once. He never went down again."
7. **Oscat offering:** "In Oscat they leave one fish on the shrine each morning, for the Tide. The old sailors swear the shore once lay much further out, before it was moved."
8. **Flame warning:** "Pray that fire comes from the hearth and not from above. Fire from above answers no prayer."
9. **The vault wind:** "In the old vaults under the hills, the Gale lives without a sky. The Wardens call it holy wind. I call it breath, and I do not like whose."
10. **A Seeker's question:** "The Arbiter speaks for the four, and the four speak for the world. Then who speaks for us? Seekers who ask this are given more chores."

### Bright Path (10)
1. **The kept flame:** "Keep the flame and the Lie cannot see you. My grandmother's grandmother kept it through the long dark. Still, the sky watched."
2. **The creed:** "Good thought, good word, good deed. Say it at dawn, at noon and at the lighting of the lamps, and the Lie will find no door into your house."
3. **Hymn of the Ordeal:** "The Lie shall have its season, the world shall burn pure, and the Bright Flame shall begin it all again. Blessed are those ready to begin again."
4. **The carried fire:** "The fire of this altar was carried from Restena in a lantern by a Candle who walked forty days. It went out once. The village does not speak of that winter."
5. **A Lantern's log:** "The flame bent north all night, though there was no wind. The Beacon says the Flame is showing us the way. I have doubled the watch."
6. **Warning to Walkers:** "If a stranger asks how long the world has turned, do not answer. Bring them to the Beacon. Questions are how the Lie gets in."
7. **The realm of the Flame:** "Beyond the last Ordeal waits the realm of the Flame, where the pure walk without shadow. The Dawnbringer has sworn Restena will be first through its gate."
8. **A good deed:** "Here a Spark gave his only coat to a stranger in the snow. The stranger thanked him in a tongue no one knew and was gone by morning. There were no tracks."
9. **Dawn prayer:** "Bright Flame, rise again tomorrow. Rise as you always have. Do not dim, as you did for the grandfathers."
10. **The Beacon's decree:** "By order of the Beacon, the tuff doorway in the hills is sealed. Whatever made it was not of the Flame, and did not need light to see."

### The Promise (8)
1. **The Book:** "And the One said: many times I have made the world, and many times I have ended it, yet always I keep a remnant. Count yourselves among the kept."
2. **The Book:** "Do not build your tower high. The towers of the fathers reached the clouds, and the clouds answered."
3. **A Shepherd's letter:** "Brothers, sisters: the Walkers have closed the well where we met. We gather now in the mill at the new moon. Bring no light. The One sees without it."
4. **The Book:** "In the last days a voice will come, and it will not be believed. Listen anyway."
5. **A Reader's note:** "I read the Book aloud tonight to six souls. Last year we were twelve. The Primas do not hunt us as the Walkers do. They only stop selling us bread."
6. **The prophecy:** "The promised one shall walk through fire, through the sea and through the endless dark, and shall stand where no one stands, and shall choose."
7. **A Shepherd's letter:** "A Listener asked why the Book speaks of 'the land before'. I told her it means the land to come. I am no longer sure which I meant."
8. **Carved in a cellar:** "Here the Promised hid for three winters. We kept the faith, we kept the Book, we kept each other. The One kept nothing for us."

### Vitera (6)
1. "They are not gods. Gods do not need to watch." (scratched under the mark)
2. "Count the mountains from this hill. Write them down and hide the page. When the world ends and begins, climb again and count. You will find the same mountains."
3. "The Walkers tend the fire, the Primas bless the wind, the Promised wait. All of them want the Sift. Only we know it is not a prize. It is a door."
4. "If an enderman stares at you, do not look back. It is trying to remember who it was."
5. "There is a dragon at the end of everything. Kill it once and you buy us time. Kill it enough times and you buy us the truth."
6. "Coreal says the world runs fast for those who watch it, like a candle burnt in a single breath. Then we are the candle."

### Unknown (6) — written in enchanting glyphs, readable with Coreal's books
1. "Twenty-fifth turn begun. They rebuilt faster than the last. Lengthen the winters."
2. "One of them reached the vaults and passed every trial. Keep her. Watch how she ages."
3. "The same land, the same rivers. Change only the people. A test needs a control."
4. "They have begun to count the turns. Let them explain it with gods. Gods are easier to watch."
5. "The crack is open again. One of them is climbing. Send the dragon."
6. "Do not let them reach the Sift. Not yet."

## Dungeon diaries (9 × 8 pages)

### Overgrown — the Brewers (Nitox) · Wenna, apprentice brewer
1. "First page of my book. Mistress Orla says a brewer writes everything down, even the failures. Today I made a cure for cough. It cured the cough and turned my hair green."
2. "Moss grows on everything here. Orla says moss is the oldest brewer of all: it turns stone into soil, given time. We have plenty of time, she says."
3. "We sell cures in the valley, poison for rats, and something for broken hearts that Orla will not let me taste."
4. "A cough has come to the valley that none of our cures can touch. It takes the old first. The goats and hens are perfectly well."
5. "Orla brewed three nights without sleep. Nothing. She says a sickness that chooses who it takes is not a sickness. It is a decision."
6. "Half the valley is gone. The animals wander the empty houses. The pigs have never been fatter. I hate them for it."
7. "Orla is coughing. She made me promise to keep writing, so whoever comes after will know the moss did not do this. Something did."
8. "I am the last in the valley, and I am coughing. The hens are laying well. I hid this book in the roots, where the moss will keep it. Brew better than we did."

### Frozen — the Frostwardens (Graciel) · Ilse, a young Frostwarden
1. "We Frostwardens keep the passes open between the valleys. Snow in winter, flowers in summer, and the ice halls cool all year for the meat."
2. "Summer festival. We carved a bear from ice for the children, and they cried when it melted. Old Hesk told them it would come back next winter."
3. "Strange: the noon light looks thin, like a lantern with its wick trimmed. Nobody else seems to notice."
4. "The sun went dim at noon, with not a cloud. Old Hesk says someone turned it down like a lantern wick."
5. "Snow in high summer. The flowers froze standing up, still open. The ice halls are full of people now, not meat."
6. "The passes are closed. We are all in the halls. Hesk says the warmth did not leave; it was taken. He says it quietly, so the children do not hear."
7. "We burn the furniture, then the doors. Strays came down from the peaks last night, and their arrows were as cold as the sky."
8. "One night. That is all it took, one night from summer to this. If you read this, keep a fire. Keep many. And watch the noon light."

### Sunken — the Tidefolk (Basylas) · Corin, guardian-keeper
1. "I have been chosen to keep the guardians! Six fat ones and an elder, all eyes and spines. They follow me through the halls like ducklings."
2. "We build in prismarine because it grows with the sea, the Elder says. A monument is never finished. It is tended."
3. "Today the Elder showed me the Heart. It is kept in the deepest room. She says it hears everything the sea hears, and never forgets."
4. "Why hide it so deep? The Elder says: so that the ones who erase things cannot find it. I asked who erases things. She told me to feed the guardians."
5. "Day 40 of the guarding. The Elder keeps the Heart below. If they cannot find it, they cannot erase what it heard."
6. "The elder guardian now curses any hand that digs near our walls. We taught it that. Better our arms grow weak than the Heart be found."
7. "The sea is pulling back from the shore, further each day, like a breath drawn in. Everyone has gone to the high halls."
8. "The breath is coming back. We have sealed the Heart and flooded the lower halls. Guardians, keep it. Whoever comes after: find the Heart, and listen."

### Deep Dark — the Deep Ones (Selince) · Sael, a gatewright
1. "We have gone deep, where the sky cannot see us. The city grows in deepslate and candlelight. Down here, we are left alone."
2. "The masters are building something at the heart of the city: a great frame, like a door with nothing behind it. They say it is a copy."
3. "A copy of what? Of the old doors in the tuff vaults, they say. Doors that were not made by anyone we know."
4. "The frame woke today. A hum, then silence, then a sound like breathing. Sculk began to grow from the floor where it hummed."
5. "We passed through. Halls of memory, room after room, every age that ever was. We call it the Archive. We whisper there; things listen."
6. "The Archive has a hall of names: everyone who ever walked the tuff trials, in every age. Some names are from ages not yet come. One of them is {player}."
7. "We were heard. The sculk spreads wherever we speak. The masters have made a guardian of the silence, blind, so nothing it sees can fool it."
8. "Do not speak in the city. Do not run. The Warden keeps what we found. We were too loud. Be quieter than us."

### Nether — the Fortress-builders (Rethen) · Varo, fortress mason
1. "We crossed the fire gate to escape the end of the world. This side is hot and red, but nothing here watches us. Or so we hope."
2. "Nether brick holds against the heat. We build along the lava sea: long halls, high walls. King Aldren says we will be safe a thousand years."
3. "The blazes are curious, not cruel. The young ones follow the builders like sparks from a forge."
4. "Scouts found ruins out here, older than ours: bridges of blackstone, halls of gold. Someone fled here before us. The land looks like the world after a fire."
5. "I begin to understand. This is not another world. This is ours, later. This is what it becomes."
6. "The king wears black now and does not sleep. He says he will rule this place even if the world above is lost."
7. "It followed us. The sky above the lava cracked open and the dark came through. No gate is far enough."
8. "Our king has become a thing with three heads and no mercy. We locked his skull in the deepest hall. If you find it, do not wake him."

### End — the Ascended (Scande) · Ione, sky-builder
1. "We came through the eye-door to the dark islands. Purpur grows here like stone flowers. We are building cities toward the stars."
2. "The shulkers took to us. They guard the towers and float our builders to the high places. Up here, falling is the only danger."
3. "A great beast circles the main island. We leave it alone, and it leaves us alone. For now."
4. "From the farthest island you can see where the dark ends. Beyond it is not more dark. It is something huge and very slow, like a face too big to see."
5. "Time is wrong out here. A candle lit at the edge burns for a day. The same candle in the city burns for an hour. Who lives that slowly?"
6. "We think we are close to them, whoever they are. The edge feels like a place never meant to be open: a crack in a wall."
7. "The islands are drifting apart. Our bridges snap one by one. The beast no longer circles. It dives."
8. "I am alone on a floating tower; the others are specks. I have seen the edge, and the edge has seen me. Do not go to the edge."

### Buried — the Kilnfolk (Belaz) · Tobias, a kiln-master's son
1. "My father fires the best bricks in the sand country, red and yellow and every colour of the dunes. Our tombs will stand forever, he says."
2. "We bury our dead under pyramids, with traps for thieves and wool for comfort. Father says a good tomb is a prayer made of brick."
3. "A long dry season. The kilns run day and night for the new temple. The heat is good for business."
4. "A star fell over the far dunes last night, slowly, as if lowered on a rope. Where it landed, the sand turned to glass."
5. "More stars. Not falling: placed. Each one starts a fire, and the fires do not go out, not even in the rain."
6. "Father says fire cannot come from the sky. Fire comes from kilns and hearths and careless hands. He says it louder every day."
7. "We have moved into the tombs, under the sand, where the fire cannot reach. The dead do not mind sharing."
8. "The air is hot even down here. Father is sealing the doors with his best bricks. The fire started in the sky, not on the ground. Remember that."

### Trial — the First Heroes (Tenrys) · Aric, one of the seven
1. "We found copper vaults under the hills, with doors that open for those who fight well. Inside are rewards, and harder doors."
2. "The breezes are fast and do not bleed. The vault keys come from the beaten. Each chamber feels like a question with a sword for an answer."
3. "We are seven now. We call ourselves heroes, half joking. The vaults seem to like it; each room is harder, as if they are measuring us."
4. "The deepest chamber has writing on its walls, in the same glyphs as the enchanting tables. We cannot read it, but it is not decoration."
5. "We have passed every chamber in the land. Last night a door opened that was not there before. Light behind it, and something waiting."
6. "They spoke to us without mouths. They said we had done well. They said there was a place for us in every turn of the world to come."
7. "Brann did not want to go. They took him anyway. The rest of us followed, because what else could we do?"
8. "If you are reading this, you walk the vaults too. They will tell you that you did well. Ask what the reward is before you accept it."

### Illager Keep — the Hollow Kings (Hergun) · Marek, royal scribe
1. "Ninth year of King Hollan's reign. The empire runs from sea to sea, and still the king hungers. A crown must grow, he says, or it shrinks."
2. "The king has taken the forests for his halls. Dark oak, every tree. The woodsmen who refused were given axes and new masters."
3. "The king's evokers have learned old words that call fangs from the ground. They say the words were found in a vault no one built."
4. "The king has heard of the eye-door, the dark islands and the crystals that heal the great beast. He means to take them, and its power with them."
5. "We marched to the end of the world, ten thousand strong. The crystals hum like the vaults. The king laughed when the first one cracked."
6. "The sky answered. At first not a dragon, but a silence. Then everything came at once: the beast, the fire, the dark."
7. "We fled back to the forests. Our faces are grey now and our tongues sour, and we cannot stand the villages we used to rule. We are less than we were."
8. "We were the Hollow Kings. Now we are raiders in our own forests. Whoever is next: they do not end us for being wicked. They end us for coming too near."
