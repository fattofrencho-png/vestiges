The history of this world is told backwards. Each dragon you kill opens a chapter, and each chapter reaches further into the past.

@lock dragon 1
Each dragon is the memory of one past cycle. Dragon 1 is the most recent cycle, and dragon 24 is the first. Each chapter tells who lived, how it ended, and gives one clue about those who end the cycles. The clues grow clearer with each act.
@endlock

@page peoples | Peoples of past cycles | History
Each past cycle had its own people. Most of them are known only from ruins and old stories. A page opens when you kill that cycle's dragon.

@entry pe1 | The Vault-builders | dragon 1
Cycle of [[ch1|dragon 1]]. They left the strongholds, whose libraries are written in enchanting-table glyphs.
Still in the world: [[st-stronghold|strongholds]], and the [[mob-silverfish|silverfish]] that guard the eye-door.

@entry pe2 | The Kilnfolk | dragon 2 or found diary-buried-1
Cycle of [[ch2|dragon 2]]. They fired bricks in the sand country and buried their dead under pyramids. Their last words were that the fire started in the sky.
Still in the world: [[st-desert-pyramid|desert pyramids]], [[mob-husk|husks]], [[mob-parched|the parched]], [[mob-camel-husk|camel husks]].
Voices: Tobias, a kiln-master's son ([[frag-diaries|Buried diary]]).

## The council of Dilecta

The three councillors are elected by the guilds, from a pool of old houses.

@entry fig-goldmere | Ambrose Goldmere
Councillor and master of money and trade. Old money, from a family that owns the harbour tolls.

@entry fig-gatewarden | Maren Gatewarden
Councillor and commander of the army. From a lesser old house. Started as a watch guard on the city gates, and never lets anyone forget it.

@entry fig-ashless | Aldys Ashless
Councillor and Beacon of Dilecta, the [[bright-path|Bright Path]]'s high priest in the capital. A shady background. He has been in the order since he was 12.

## Heads of the faiths

@entry fig-evane | Evane Lightsworn
The Dawnbringer, head of the [[bright-path|Bright Path]], who sits in [[restena|Restena]]. A former Lantern from a poor temple, chosen for her fierce faith. She has sworn Restena will be first through the gate of the realm of the Flame.

@entry fig-tessar | Arbiter Tessar
Head of the [[primacy|Primacy]], speaking for all four Powers. The 4 Elders choose one of their own for life, and he sits in [[thornner|Thornner]], the faith's old home. His title comes from an old word for "four". He has no family name, and his old name is secret.

@entry fig-elders | The four Elders of the Primacy
**Flame:** Ignace Ashvale. **Tide:** Merrin Deepwater. **Stone:** Garth Rockhold. **Gale:** Tobin Highwind. Each speaks for one Power of the [[primacy|Primacy]].

## Rulers of past ages

@entry fig-hollan | King Hollan | dragon 12 or found diary-keep-1
Ruler of the [[pe12|Hollow Kings]]' empire, which ran from sea to sea. "A crown must grow, he says, or it shrinks." He took the forests for his halls, and marched ten thousand strong to the end of the world for the crystals.
Source: [[frag-diaries|Illager Keep diary]], written by his scribe Marek.

@entry fig-aldren | King Aldren | dragon 17 or found diary-nether-2
The last king of the [[pe17|Fortress-builders]]. He promised his people a thousand safe years, then wore black and stopped sleeping. He became [[mob-wither|the Wither]], and his people locked his skull in the deepest hall.
Source: [[frag-diaries|Nether diary]], written by his mason Varo.

@gap The rulers of the Two Crowns, the Windward towers and every other cycle have no names yet.

## Diary writers and the people they knew

@entry fig-wenna | Wenna and Mistress Orla | found diary-overgrown-1
Apprentice and master among the [[pe9|Brewers]]. Orla brewed three nights without sleep against the cough, and called it "not a sickness. It is a decision." Wenna was the last in the valley, and hid her book in the roots.

@entry fig-ilse | Ilse and Old Hesk | found diary-frozen-1
A young [[pe8|Frostwarden]] and the elder who told the children the ice bear would come back. It was Hesk who said someone turned the sun down like a lantern wick.

@entry fig-corin | Corin and the Elder | found diary-sunken-1
A guardian-keeper of the [[pe16|Tidefolk]], and the Elder who kept the Heart below.

@entry fig-sael | Sael | found diary-deep-1
A gatewright of the [[pe18|Deep Ones]], who passed through the frame into the Archive.

@entry fig-varo | Varo | found diary-nether-1
A fortress mason of the [[pe17|Fortress-builders]], and the one who understood what the Nether is.

@entry fig-ione | Ione | found diary-end-1
A sky-builder of the [[pe13|Ascended]], last seen alone on a floating tower.

@entry fig-tobias | Tobias | found diary-buried-1

@entry fig-aric | Aric and Brann | found diary-trial-1

@entry fig-marek | Marek | found diary-keep-1
