@entry place-names | How everything else is named
The mod reads your world and groups big areas into named places: mountain ranges, forests, deserts, seas, rivers and landmasses. They are named as you approach. Names show as entry titles, map labels, a Places page in the Codex, and in villager backgrounds. Vanilla structures keep their names. Ruined shrines carry their own name and fragment. Players can rename places later.

## Beyond the world

@entry archive | The Archive
The place behind the Gate, the tuff and copper frame that opens with a key. Each dungeon inside is themed.
@lock dragon 18
Dungeons are the world's archive. In a way, inside them we relive events from the past.

The Archive holds every Hero tested in the trial chambers, including your name, written before you were born.
@endlock

@entry threshold | The Threshold
The arena in the End, where you fight the dragons.

@entry vestige | The Vestige | dragon 1
A very big island far out in the End, very low in the End. Every end gateway leads there, and a return gateway goes back to the arena. No mob spawns on the island.

It holds a lore hall: the Entrance, 4 wings (one per act) with 6 alcoves each (one per dragon), and a sealed centre. After each kill, that dragon's alcove rebuilds itself over a few seconds, with a lectern holding the chapter as a book, a mural of that cycle, and the dragon's head on a pedestal. Act bosses (6, 12, 18) open the next wing. You may build freely on and around the island, outside the hall's protected space. Its waystone was placed by the first build, and trips to it always cost 1 emerald.

@entry sift | The Sift
A legendary place, rumoured to be reachable. Some say it is another world, some say it is what the world was, or what it could be. Every faith wants to conquer it, each sure it is its own promised realm.

## Urban Governance & Leadership
@bible The Ruling Council (Triumvirate): The three high councilors holding supreme executive and legislative authority over the city.
@bible Councilors' Aides & Secretaries: Scribes, personal heralds, and administrative assistants managing daily decrees.
@bible Master of the Mint: High official responsible for currency coinage, treasury management, and city taxes.
@bible Chancellor of the Exchequer / Auditor: Bureaucrats tracking state expenditure, tariffs, and guild dues.
@bible Magistrates & Judges: Jurists presiding over civil disputes, petty crimes, and high-court trials.

## Military & Security Forces
@bible Lord Commander of the Army: General officer directing garrison troops, external defense, and military campaigns.
@bible Regimental Captains & Lieutenants: Officers commanding infantry, cavalry, or siege engineer units within the royal army.
@bible City Watch Commander (Castellan/Captain of the Watch): Head of urban law enforcement responsible for night patrols, gates, and curfews.
@bible Watchmen & Beat Patrols: Ordinary guards manning the walls, gates, and city districts to deter crime.
@bible Private Bodyguards & Retainers: Elite armsmen sworn directly to individual members of the ruling council.
@bible Bailiffs & Wardens: Specialized officers collecting fines, executing warrants, and overseeing specific city wards.

## Religious Hierarchies (Two Major, One Minor)
@bible High Prelates / Arch-Priests: Supreme spiritual leaders of the two major faiths, wielding vast political and moral influence.
@bible Dogmatic Inquisitors / Zealots: Religious enforcers hunting heresy, unapproved spellcasting, and corruption.
@bible Temple Priests, Curates & Clerks: Officiants managing daily rites, marriages, funerals, and temple tithes.
@bible Faith Militant / Templar Knights: Dedicated holy orders serving the major religions as armed defenders.
@bible Heretical Cult Acolytes / Secret Custodians: Underground operatives preserving the tenets of the minor, marginalized faith.
@bible Alms-Givers & Hospitalers: Monastic caretakers running soup kitchens, plague wards, and orphanages.

## Commerce, Guilds & Crafts
@bible Guild Masters: Heads of influential trade associations (blacksmiths, weavers, masons) regulating prices and apprentices.
@bible Merchant Princes & Caravan Factors: Traders handling bulk imports, exports, and high-value luxury goods.
@bible Master Masons & Architects: Engineers directing stone fortifications, cathedral building, and structural repairs.
@bible Alchemists & Apothecaries: Brewers of potions, healers, and compounders of chemical reagents or explosives.
@bible Scribes, Illuminators & Printers: Producers of legal documents, scrolls, and early printed texts.
@bible Innkeepers, Taverners & Brothel Keepers: Proprietors managing the city's hospitality and entertainment districts.

## Municipal Infrastructure & Underworld
@bible Master of Works / City Engineer: Overseer of aqueducts, sewers, bridges, and public sanitation.
@bible Executioner / Torturer: Municipal official carrying out capital punishment and interrogations.
@bible Rat-Catchers & Night-Soil Men: Laborers clearing waste, vermin, and biological hazards from the streets.
@bible Smuggler Bosses & Fence Operators: Underworld figures running illicit black markets, contraband, and bribes.
@bible Thieves' Guild Enforcers / Cutpurses: Outlaws operating in the shadows of the city's poorest wards.

@entry primacy | The Primacy
The elemental faith. Its followers are called **Primas**. There are four Powers: Flame, Tide, Stone and Gale. The cycle is "the Turning", the elements washing the world clean when people grow proud.
**Ranks:** Seeker → Initiate → Keeper (sworn to one element, such as Keeper of the Tide) → Warden (runs a shrine) → Elder (4, one per element) → the Arbiter (speaks for all four).
Texts: [[frag-shrines|the Primacy shrines]]. People: [[hero-aveline|Aveline Saltmere]], [[hero-brannoc|Brannoc Deepdelve]], [[hero-mael|Mael Stormwake]], [[hero-garrick|Garrick Thornwood]], [[fig-maud|Elder Maud]].
Its Elders: [[fig-elders|the four Elders]].
Its head: [[fig-tessar|Arbiter Tessar]].

@entry bright-path | The Bright Path
The faith of light. Its followers are called **Walkers**. The Bright Flame stands against the Lie, with eternal temple fires and the creed "good thought, good word, good deed". The cycle is "the Ordeal".
**Ranks:** Spark → Candle → Lantern (keeps a temple fire) → Beacon (high priest of a city) → the Dawnbringer (head of the faith, in Restena).
Texts: [[frag-shrines|the Bright Path shrines]]. People: [[hero-corvin|Corvin Ironvow]], [[hero-odo|Odo Archwright]], [[hero-ysolde|Ysolde Brightwick]].
Its head: [[fig-evane|Evane Lightsworn]], the Dawnbringer.

@entry promise | The Promise
The small faith. Its followers are called **the Promised**. The One promised to save a faithful few, through prophets and a Book, and they wait for a promised one. The cycle is "the Judgment". It is tolerated but bullied almost everywhere, and many of its circles meet in hiding.
**Ranks:** Listener → Believer → Reader (may read the Book aloud) → Shepherd (leads a hidden circle) → the Voice (a prophet; nobody knows if one lives today).
Texts: [[frag-shrines|the Promise shrines]].
