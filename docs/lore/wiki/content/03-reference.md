## Who are the makers?

@entry kesmar | The Kesmar
The makers. A higher race that uses our world as a testing ground. Their time runs differently: to them we are an accelerated simulation, and they can modify the world as they want. They hide their true form and try to kill you with a dragon, the same dragon they unleashed on the world again and again, starting a new cycle each time.

**Still unknown, even now:** what makes them launch a new cycle, and why some cycles last longer than others. No exact dates or durations exist outside this cycle, because most records are destroyed. "15,000 years" is what people believe, not a fact.

@entry who-knows | Who knows what
The three faiths don't know the truth, and read the cycle through their own beliefs: gods keeping, showing or proving their power, punishing or saving the people. A cult hunted by the faiths, the [[vitera|Vitera]], does know. You are an ordinary person, who may or may not slowly learn the truth, depending on your goals. Someone who stops at dragon 1 and only does dungeons never learns the full truth.

@entry bible-truth | The world's first description
These are the author's own words.
@bible The 15,000-Year Cycle: The world functions as an enduring, artificial simulation that undergoes periodic civilization purges and global memory-wiping events. Inhabitants rebuild without knowing their true origin.
@bible The 24 Dragons: The central imperative of the simulation requires slaying the Ender Dragon 24 separate times to unlock progression or reach the world's ultimate conclusion.
@bible Vestiges of the Past: Ancient, advanced remnants persist across cycles, leaving functional artifacts and relics that outlive the memories of the people who built them.

@entry endings | The two endings
After dragon 24, an altar stands at the centre of the [[vestige|Vestige]] and you choose. Your save file is never touched, whichever you pick.

**Unmake:** an ending scene, an end poem, and the title "Cyclebreaker". The world keeps running as a fading echo, and the Echo dragons are its last memories breaking apart. The hall fades grey and ghostly.

**Reign:** an ending scene and the title "Sovereign". You replace the higher race as master, with powers at your Capital's Bell: set time, set weather, change the next dungeon theme. The Echo dragons are the makers trying to take the world back. The centre of the hall becomes a throne room where the master powers also work.

## Points that disagree

1. **15,000 years.** The design log still says "a piece of lore from the 15,000-year cycle", while the lore log says that number is only a belief. The main chat should reword it at the next hand-over.

## Lock choices I made, to confirm

- **Flavour (level 2) is open in the wiki.** You only set a lock for Lore (level 3), and the Codex shows its own lines on its own schedule.
- **The name "Kesmar" and the world's true description** stay on The truth page behind dragon 24.
- **Peoples** open at their dragon, or earlier if you find that people's diary.
- **Fragments** open only when found, so the game must also tell the wiki which ones you found.
- **Mother Elsbeth's secret faith** opens at dragon 24.
- **Dungeons as the world's archive** opens at dragon 18, with the Archive reveal.
- **Structure stories** open at the dragon of the people who built them.
