# Mob origins: drafts (NOT decisions)

> Reviewed on 2026-10-30. The approved choices were moved to `mob-origins.md`. This file is only a record of the options.

Everything here is a **proposal** for the author. Nothing counts until it is approved; approved cards move to `mob-origins.md`.
Already approved there: **Zombie**, **Zombie Villager**.

How to read it:
- **Now** = the current Codex lines (kept as they are). **Seen** = where and when the mob shows up in vanilla (to check in game before the Codex milestone).
- **L1** Fluff (what people think) · **L2** Flavour (a hint) · **L3** Lore (the truth, only after dragon 24, in poetry). **A / B** are options; mix freely. `-` means no text proposed.
- Each group starts with its **group idea** and the **questions** that change the texts. Answer those first, the rest follows.

## 1. Zombie kin and the Allay

**Group idea (decided for Zombie):** one curse from the Kesmar for a Sin; the look depends on where the Sin was done. Mounts are cursed because they stayed with a sinner.
**Questions**
1. Do beasts share the curse of the rider they would not leave? (Proposal: yes. It is why horse, nautilus and camel are cursed.)
2. Drowned: sailors who sinned at sea, or Tidefolk (Basylas), as the Codex says "never stopped guarding"? (A: both; the Tidefolk guarded, and some sinned by touching what they guarded. B: only sailors; the Tidefolk line is a folk mistake.)
3. Allay: from the far future, or from the Sift (the world that could be if the Kesmar are stopped)?

#### Husk
Now: "A desert zombie that does not burn by day, and leaves you hungry." · "Kilnfolk tombs keep their dead. Not all of them."
Seen: deserts, day and night.
- L1: **A** "Nobody buries their dead in the dunes any more. The sand gives them back." · **B** "Desert folk say the husk walks because the sand would not take its bones."
- L2: **A** "Day does not burn it. It was judged under this very sky, and the sky has nothing left to say." · **B** "The sun spares the husk, as if it were the desert's own."
- L3: **A** "The Kesmar judge a Sin where it is done. The husk was judged under the dune sun, and the dry has kept it hungry as the sand ever since." · **B** "Its Sin was done in the open sand, so its sentence is served there. The sun cannot burn what the desert already has."

#### Drowned
Now: "Zombies of the deep. Some throw tridents; some hold nautilus shells." · "The Tidefolk, the stories say, never stopped guarding."
Seen: oceans, rivers, underwater ruins, mostly at night.
- L1: **A** "Sailors do not swim at night. The sea keeps a count, and it is not yet full." · **B** "Fishers say the drowned walk the seabed looking for the boat they never reached."
- L2: **A** "The tide never takes them. It only holds them, like a hand that will not open." · **B** "The trident is not stolen. It is the one thing the sea let them keep."
- L3: **A** "A Sin done at sea is judged at sea. The Kesmar did not drown them; they forbade the water to let go." · **B** "They touched what the sea was set to keep. The Kesmar left them there to guard it until it forgave them."

#### Zombie Horse
Now: "A horse that came back wrong." · "Ridden by the dead on dark plains, travellers say."
Seen: rare, carrying a rider (check in game).
- L1: **A** "A good horse never leaves its rider. Travellers say that is the whole of the tragedy." · **B** "Plains folk bar the stable on moonless nights, and do not say why."
- L2: **A** "It carries a rider who cannot stop." · **B** "It is the only dead thing that looks sorry."
- L3: **A** "A beast has no Sin. It was cursed for the rider it would not leave." · **B** "The curse takes what is bound to a sinner. The horse had only the misfortune of being loved."

#### Zombie Nautilus
Now: "A rotting nautilus, ridden by drowned in the deep." · "Even the sea's mounts don't rest in peace."
Seen: deep ocean, under a drowned rider.
- L1: "Drowned captains ride them the way kings ride horses, and no one has heard them give an order."
- L2: "It follows the drowned as a hound follows a hand."
- L3: **A** "Like the horse, it carried a sinner once, and the sea was told to keep them both." · **B** "The sea's mounts were loyal. The sea is not kind to loyalty."

#### Camel Husk
Now: "A camel long dead, still walking the dunes." · "The Kilnfolk buried their camels beside them. Some never stayed buried."
Seen: deserts, under a husk rider.
- L1: **A** "Caravan drivers count their camels at dusk and again at dawn, and say nothing of the one too many." · **B** "The dunes give back what the Kilnfolk put in them, and a camel is not too proud to return."
- L2: "It walks the old caravan roads, as if it still had a load to deliver."
- L3: "Buried beside a sinner, it was cursed beside a sinner. The desert keeps its servants as it keeps its dead."

#### Allay
Now: "Hand it an item and it gathers more of the same, bringing them to a note block you play." · "Freed from pillager cages, it hums as if it remembers other cages."
Seen: caged at pillager outposts and woodland mansions; peaceful.
- L1: **A** "Children call them fairies, and leave a coin on the sill for luck." · **B** "Villagers call them little spirits, and say they like what they like for no reason."
- L2: **A** "It drifts toward what you hold, as though it already knew you would need more." · **B** "It hums a tune nobody has written yet."
- L3: **A** "They are not spirits. They are echoes of a day that has not come, slipped past the Kesmar's watch to leave us small gifts." · **B** "Something in a far tomorrow reached back, against the Kesmar's wish, and let its kindness fall here as light."

## 2. Skeletons

**Group idea:** **A** the older the Sin, the less is left. A zombie is recent, a skeleton is the oldest (flesh gone); the variants follow where the Sin was done (snow, swamp, desert, Nether). **B** skeletons are a different thing: archers of old armies never released from their post.
**Questions**
1. A or B?
2. "Its arrows never run out": because the Kesmar's debt is never paid? (Proposal: yes.)
3. Was the Brewers' alchemy (Nitox) the Sin of the swamp, which also touches Bogged and Witch?

#### Skeleton
Now: "Bones and a bow. It burns at dawn and runs from wolves." · "Its arrows never run out. Neither does its spite."
Seen: dark places, caves, dungeons, at night; burns in sunlight; flees wolves.
- L1: **A** "A zombie still walks like a man. A skeleton only remembers how to aim." · **B** "Shepherds say the bones are older than the hills, and carry a grudge older still."
- L2: **A** "Its arrows never run out, as though someone were still paying for them." · **B** "Wolves it flees. Somewhere in the bone it remembers what comes for bones."
- L3: **A** "The oldest Sins left only bones. The Kesmar forget no debt, and they never let the bow fall." · **B** "When a Sin is old, the flesh forgives itself and leaves. The bone stays to be punished."

#### Stray
Now: "A frozen skeleton whose arrows slow you." · "The last Frostwarden diary speaks of arrows as cold as the sky."
Seen: snowy plains and frozen biomes.
- L1: **A** "Snow folk say a stray is a lost archer who walked into the white and never found a hearth." · **B** "Hunters on the snow count their arrows twice, and watch for a cold one."
- L2: "Its arrows are cold as the sky, and the sky had a hand in it."
- L3: **A** "The sentence was served where the sun was turned down, and the cold became the Sin's own mark." · **B** "A Sin done in the frost is judged in the frost. They were left standing in the dark of it."

#### Bogged
Now: "A swamp skeleton grown over with mushrooms. Its arrows poison." · "Shear it for mushrooms. It does not seem to notice."
Seen: swamps and mangrove swamps.
- L1: **A** "Swamp folk say the marsh makes a garden of anyone who hides in it too long." · **B** "A bogged bone is a garden that has learned to aim."
- L2: "Its arrows poison. The swamp remembers the Brewers' cups."
- L3: "A Sin of brewing was done in the swamp, and the bog took the penance as its own garden." (needs question 3)

#### Parched
Now: "A sun-dried skeleton that shoots tipped arrows." · "Desert travellers swear it was once a Kilnfolk archer."
Seen: deserts. (New in 26.3: check mechanics in game.)
- L1: "Caravans tie a cloth over the one bone that always follows them."
- L2: "It is thirstier than the dunes, and the dunes have had centuries of practice."
- L3: "A Sin done under the open sun dried it to the bone. Its arrows carry the sun's own sting."

#### Wither Skeleton
Now: "Tall and black; its blade withers. Sometimes drops its skull." · "They still guard the halls of a king who lost his mind."
Seen: Nether fortresses.
- L1: **A** "Fortress crews say the black ones stand because no one told them the king is gone." · **B** "A wither skeleton never looks at you. It looks at the door behind you."
- L2: "Its blade rots, as the king's court did, slowly and politely."
- L3: **A** "The guard of Rethen's last king, who looked into the Nether and saw his own kingdom. They stand for it still." · **B** "They guard a future that was shown, not lived. The Wither was their king."

#### Skeleton Horse
Now: "Comes with the lightning, carrying four riders." · "Tamed after the fight, it swims well and fears nothing."
Seen: thunderstorms, as a skeleton trap.
- L1: "Lightning does not choose its roof. Sometimes it chooses a rider."
- L2: "The storm brings them. Thunder is how the oldest debts are called."
- L3: "When the sky speaks loudly, the Kesmar call their longest unpaid debts to a gallop." (link to Morst: "the makers' breath")

## 3. Creeper, spiders, slime, silverfish

**Questions**
1. Creeper: **A** a tool of the Kesmar that erases what grows too proud (links to "broken things regrew overnight, as if edited") or **B** an alchemist's failed work that learned to want (links to the Sin of alchemy)?
2. Cats fear nothing but are feared by creepers and phantoms: because cats can see the Kesmar's tools? (Proposal: yes.)
3. Spiders: left behind by the Deepdelvers (Mortre), who kept them for silk? (Proposal: yes. Plain wildlife that the dark taught to hunt is the other choice.)
4. Slime: leftover of the world's "edits" (Dhars)? (Proposal: yes. It also explains why slime chunks never move.)

#### Creeper
Now: "Silent, then hissing, then gone, along with your wall." · "Cats terrify it. Nobody has ever learned why."
Seen: surface at night and in the dark; explodes; drops gunpowder.
- L1: **A** "Builders say a creeper is the only guest who leaves the room larger than he found it." · **B** "Nobody has heard a creeper arrive. Only what it left."
- L2: **A** "It fears cats, which see what hides in the sky." · **B** "It does not hate your house. It cannot stand anything that stays built."
- L3: **A** "A tool of the Kesmar, made to erase what grows too proud. Cats see it for what it is, and it knows." · **B** "Where the alchemists failed at making life, the powder learned to want. It still goes looking for them."

#### Spider
Now: "Climbs walls and turns hostile in the dark." · "A spider ridden by a skeleton is the rarest bad luck."
Seen: surface at night and caves; calm in daylight.
- L1: **A** "Miners hang a lamp at every post. They say a spider cannot hate what it can see." · **B** "A spider in daylight is only a neighbour. At dusk it remembers it is a hunter."
- L2: "Its webs hang in neat rows, as if someone once taught it to."
- L3: **A** "The Deepdelvers kept them for their silk. When the shafts fell silent, the spiders kept working." · **B** "They are not cursed, only abandoned. The dark taught them what the miners left unsaid."

#### Cave Spider
Now: "Small, quick and poisonous. Found around spawners in mineshafts." · "Burn the cobwebs first. They always come from the webs."
Seen: abandoned mineshafts, around spawners.
- L1: "Miners keep a canary for the gas and a torch for the small ones."
- L2: "Its poison is the miners' old fear, grown teeth."
- L3: "The mineshafts held the silk farms, and the small line never left them."

#### Slime
Now: "Bounces in swamps by moonlight, and in certain chunks underground." · "Slime chunks never move. The Seers called that proof the map never changes."
Seen: swamps at night; deep underground in fixed chunks; splits when hit.
- L1: **A** "Swamp children chase them with buckets. The slimes find this very funny." · **B** "A slime is the ground's cough. It only wants to be somewhere else."
- L2: **A** "It splits when struck, as if the world could not decide how many of it there should be." · **B** "It lives where the world was patched. The seam never quite closed."
- L3: **A** "Where the Kesmar edited the world, a little of the edit stayed behind and learned to bounce." · **B** "Slime is the world's own stitching, left loose where something was taken out and put back."

#### Silverfish
Now: "Hides inside stone in strongholds and mountains. Hit one and the rest wake." · "The vault-builders guarded the eye-door with pests, it seems."
Seen: strongholds, mountain stone.
- L1: "Masons tap a stone twice before they trust it."
- L2: "-" (the Codex hint already works)
- L3: "Bred to guard Tulva's door, the line outlived the door and the builders, still faithful, still hungry."

## 5. Illagers and the Witch

**Group idea:** the illagers are the remnants of the Hollow Kings' people (chapter Hergun: they stole at the crystals). The Kesmar took their crown, home and name, and left the habit of taking.
**Questions**
1. Do all illagers (pillager, vindicator, evoker) share that origin? (Proposal: yes.)
2. Vex: **A** a word of the Kesmar's tongue given a body, or **B** the unfinished dead called back while the evoker holds the spell?
3. Witch: heirs of the Brewers, whose alchemy was the Sin? (Same as skeleton question 3.)

#### Pillager
Now: "Crossbow raider. Its captains drop ominous bottles that bring raids." · "Grey faces, sour tongues: what is left of an empire, a scribe wrote."
Seen: outposts, patrols, raids, woodland mansions.
- L1: **A** "Villagers say pillagers take what they cannot make, and cannot make anything." · **B** "A pillager hates a village for the same reason a beggar hates a bakery."

- L3: **A** "The last of the Hollow Kings' people. The kings stole at the crystals. The Kesmar took their crown, their home and their name, and left the hunger." · **B** "Their empire fell on the day it learned too much. What remains forgot the lesson and kept the hunger."

Seen: woodland mansions, raids, patrols.
- L1: **A** "Vindicator means 'one who proves a point'. They have only ever had the one point." · **B** "A vindicator never raids. He collects."
- L2: "It carries an axe where a king once carried a pen."

#### Evoker

Seen: woodland mansions, raids.
- L1: **A** "Illager priests, villagers say, who speak a tongue they never learned." · **B** "An evoker's chanting is the only sound a village hears and cannot place."

- L3: **A** "The spells were copied from the walls of a trial chamber, where the Kesmar wrote the rules of this world. It reads aloud what it was never meant to read." · **B** "Kings who steal knowledge also steal the words. The evoker is the last who can still say them."

#### Vex

Seen: summoned by evokers.
- L1: "Wherever an evoker stands, the air gets crowded."

#### Witch

Seen: swamp huts, raids.
- L1: **A** "Swamp children dare each other to knock. Nobody has knocked twice." · **B** "A witch is only a brewer nobody trusts."
- L2: "She drinks her own work, so she knows the cure and will not share it."

## 6. The Nether

**Group idea:** the Nether is what the world becomes if the Kesmar continue. So its creatures are either **A** what the overworld's people and beasts become in that future, or **B** natives of a place that was made later. Anything that crosses to the overworld is read by the same law as the cursed dead, which would explain zoglins and zombified piglins.
**Questions**
1. A or B for piglins and hoglins?
2. Ghasts: the sparks of the fire the Kilnfolk saw start in the sky (chapter Belaz)? (Proposal: yes.)
3. Happy Ghast: a hint of the Sift, what the Nether could have been? (Proposal: yes.)
4. Zoglin and zombified piglin: crossing over means the Kesmar's law reads them like the dead? (Proposal: yes.)

#### Blaze
Now: "Spins and spits fire in Nether fortresses. Snowballs hurt it." · "The fortress-builders called them sparks from a forge, and followed them home."
Seen: Nether fortresses, spawners.
- L1: **A** "Fortress crews say a blaze is a spark that forgot to go out." · **B** "Nobody has seen a blaze rest."
- L2: "Cold unmakes it, as if it were a fire borrowed from somewhere warmer than here."
- L3: **A** "Sparks of the great fire that began in the sky, and fell through the world to land below." · **B** "The Nether's forge never cooled. Blazes are what it throws off while it waits for the world to arrive."

#### Ghast
Now: "Wails and fires exploding fireballs. Hit them back." · "Fortress diaries call them the crying sky."
Seen: Nether wastes, soul sand valleys.
- L1: **A** "Travellers who come back say the ghasts weep for the sky they lost." · **B** "A ghast cries before it kills. Most things do it after."
- L2: "Its tears burn. Its fireballs can be sent back, as if sorrow could be returned."
- L3: **A** "The fire in the sky that the Kilnfolk saw, still falling, still wailing." · **B** "The grief of a world that never got to be, floating over the lava."

#### Happy Ghast
Now: "Raised from a dried ghast. Fit it with a harness and fly." · "Proof the Nether can be gentle, if you keep it wet."
Seen: from a dried ghast, in the overworld.
- L1: "Children call them clouds with manners."
- L2: "It was never meant to be fierce. The dry made it angry."
- L3: "A glimpse of the Sift: what the Nether might have been if no one had meant it to burn."

#### Piglin
Now: "Trades for gold, and attacks anyone not wearing some." · "Bastions were built by someone before them, the fortress diaries say."
Seen: Nether, bastion remnants.
- L1: **A** "Merchants joke that a piglin sells anything and buys only gold." · **B** "Wear one gold boot and you are a guest. Wear none and you are dinner."
- L2: "Their trade is older than their tongue. They remember the exchange, not the words."
- L3: **A** "The heirs of the bastion builders' servants. They kept their masters' love of gold and forgot the masters." · **B** "A people that took the Kesmar's gold as a sign of favour, and have worshipped it since."

#### Piglin Brute
Now: "Guards bastion treasure with a golden axe. Gold won't calm it." · "It never trades. It remembers what gold is for."
Seen: bastion remnants.
- L1: "The brutes never smile and never sell."
- L2: "Its axe is gold, and so is its anger."
- L3: "The bastion's guard, made not to bargain, because the treasure was never theirs to spend."

#### Zombified Piglin
Now: "Peaceful until you hit one. Then all of them remember." · "A piglin brought to the overworld becomes this. The Nether doesn't travel well."
Seen: Nether wastes, and from lightning-struck pigs / piglins in the overworld.
- L1: "Strike one and the whole herd takes it personally."
- L2: "It carries its golden sword like a prayer."
- L3: "A Nether thing crossing the edge is read by the law that reads the dead, and found wanting."

#### Hoglin
Now: "Charges through crimson forests. Warped fungus makes it back away." · "Brought to the overworld, it turns into a zoglin."
Seen: crimson forests, bastions.
- L1: "Piglins breed them like cattle and fear them like kings."
- L2: "Warped fungus scares it. It remembers a gentler wood."
